Voice of customer

What customers actually say

Gathered from third-party reviews, forums and video — the site’s own pages are excluded. Every quote was verified word-for-word against the page it links to; anything that could not be verified was discarded rather than paraphrased.

Said before buying — these are conversion problems

Hidden fees revealed late in checkout
The user is enticed with a low advertised price. After investing time and effort, they discover unexpected fees and charges when they reach the checkout.
— deceptive.design
Prospects are deterred when unexpected costs appear after they've invested time, leading to distrust and cart abandonment.
Misleading information damages trust
Misleading information: the customer feels they were sold something different from what they got. Damages trust faster than almost anything else
— wisernotify.com
Customers feel deceived when the product or service differs from what was described, eroding confidence in the brand.
Unclear refund policies create hesitation
If your refund policy is unclear, it can harm customer experience and make your roadmap for processing requests difficult.
— squarespace.com
Ambiguous return and refund terms make prospects uncertain about post-purchase recourse, reducing willingness to buy.
Junk fees inflate final price
Junk fees are unexpected, hidden, or mandatory charges added to the advertised price of a product or service, often revealed late in the checkout process or after the purchase.
— alliantcreditunion.org
Unexpected mandatory charges make it hard to compare prices and can significantly increase the total cost, causing frustration.

Said after buying — these shape what the page must reassure

Lack of communication after purchase
Lack of communication: no updates, no replies, no follow-up. Customers who feel invisible write the most frustrated reviews, often over issues that were fixable
— wisernotify.com
Customers feel ignored when there are no updates or replies, leading to frustration and negative reviews.
Poor customer service unresolved complaints
Customer service complaints: someone on your team was rude, slow, or unhelpful, and the customer didn’t get resolution.
— wisernotify.com
Rude, slow, or unhelpful service leaves customers without resolution, damaging loyalty and prompting negative feedback.
